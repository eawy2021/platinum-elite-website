Damage, Cleaning & Incidentals Policy

To ensure a premium and safe travel experience, all passengers are expected to treat our vehicles and chauffeurs with respect. The following policy applies to every reservation.

1. Client Responsibility

The booking client is fully responsible for the behavior of all passengers. Any damage, loss, or additional cleaning required as a result of the reservation will be billed to the booking client.

2. Damage Charges

Repair or replacement costs will be invoiced for any damage to the vehicle, including but not limited to:

  • Torn, stained, or burned upholstery
  • Broken fixtures or missing accessories
  • Scratches, dents, or vandalism
  • Damage to in-vehicle electronics or lighting

3. Cleaning Fees

Additional cleaning fees may be applied if the vehicle requires extra detailing beyond normal use. Fees may be assessed for:

  • Food or beverage spills
  • Excessive trash or debris
  • Biohazard cleanup (vomit, blood, etc.)
  • Smoke or strong odor removal

4. Prohibited Items

Smoking, vaping, and illegal substances are strictly prohibited inside all vehicles. Violations will result in a minimum $250 cleaning fee, plus repair or downtime charges if applicable.

5. Invoicing & Payment

All damage or cleaning charges will be documented with photos and driver notes. Clients will receive an official Square invoice, payable upon receipt. Non-payment may result in collection actions and/or refusal of future service.

6. Vehicle Downtime

If damage or cleanup renders a vehicle unavailable for scheduled service, the client may be invoiced for lost revenue in addition to repair or cleaning costs.
